# Flux

An AI assistant for Adobe After Effects. Connect your ChatGPT or Claude account, describe what you want, and work directly with your compositions, layers and animations.

- Create and edit with plain-language instructions.
- Stay inside After Effects.
- Customise your accent colour.
- Get update notices inside Flux.

## Download and install

[Download the latest release](https://github.com/paras582/flux-updates/releases/latest).

1. Save your work and close After Effects.
2. Install the downloaded `.zxp` with [ZXP Installer](https://aescripts.com/learn/post/zxp-installer/).
3. Reopen After Effects and go to **Window → Extensions → Flux**.
4. Connect your account in **Settings → Account**.

Requires After Effects 2022 or newer. One download serves Mac and Windows; Claude sign-in also requires [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/setup).

For future updates, open **Settings → Updates** in Flux.
